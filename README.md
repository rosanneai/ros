## Requirements

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon or an Intel processor.

## Install

1. Download [ROS.dmg](https://github.com/rosanneai/ros/releases/latest/download/ROS.dmg).
2. Open it and drag ROS into Applications.
3. Open ROS from your Applications folder.

ROS is signed by its developer and notarized by Apple.

## Updates

ROS keeps itself up to date. It checks for a new version twice a day, downloads it in the background and installs it when you quit, so it never interrupts what you're doing. To check right away, choose ROS › Check for Updates…. Automatic updates can be turned off in Settings › General.

Every version is listed, with what changed, on the [Releases](https://github.com/rosanneai/ros/releases) page.

## Privacy

Trackers are blocked from the start, and your history and bookmarks stay on your Mac, encrypted. Checking for updates sends only the version of ROS you have. Read more at [rosanne.ai/privacy](https://rosanne.ai/privacy/).
